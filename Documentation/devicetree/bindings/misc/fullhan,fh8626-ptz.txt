Fullhan FH8626 pan-tilt motors

Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626-ptz"
- h-gpios: four GPIO specifiers for the horizontal phases
- v-gpios: four GPIO specifiers for the vertical phases
- h-upbound: horizontal travel limit
- v-upbound: vertical travel limit
- max-pps: maximum step rate, in pulses per second

Optional properties:
- h-invert-dir: invert the horizontal direction
- sequential-axes: move one axis at a time

A board comment may name the wired GPIOs.

Example:

	ptz-motors {
		compatible = "fullhan,fh8626-ptz";
		h-gpios = <&gpio1 18 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio1 19 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio0 3 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio0 6 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>;
		v-gpios = <&gpio1 29 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio0 5 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio0 24 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>,
			  <&gpio0 19 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>;
		h-upbound = <496>;
		v-upbound = <169>;
		max-pps = <500>;
		h-invert-dir;
		sequential-axes;
	};
