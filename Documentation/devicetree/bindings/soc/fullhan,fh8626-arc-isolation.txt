Fullhan FH8626 ARC companion isolation

The node holds the companion in reset and gates its clock. It has
no reg property.

Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626-arc-isolation",
  "fullhan,fh8626v100-arc"
- clocks: the companion clock
- clock-names: "arc"

Example:

	arc-isolation {
		compatible = "fullhan,fh8626-arc-isolation",
			     "fullhan,fh8626v100-arc";
		clocks = <&clk FH8626_CLK_ARC>;
		clock-names = "arc";
	};
