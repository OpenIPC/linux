Fullhan FH8626V100 SAR ADC

Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626v100-adc"
- reg: MMIO window of the converter
- interrupts: converter interrupt
- clocks: the ADC clock
- clock-names: "sadc"

Example:

	adc@f1200000 {
		compatible = "fullhan,fh8626v100-adc";
		reg = <0xf1200000 0x4000>;
		interrupts = <25>;
		clocks = <&clk FH8626_CLK_SADC>;
		clock-names = "sadc";
	};
