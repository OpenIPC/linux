Fullhan FH8626V100 video encoder

Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626-pae", "fullhan,fh8626v100-encoder"
- reg: three MMIO ranges, in the order of reg-names
- reg-names: "core", "buffer", "sram"
- interrupts: encoder interrupt
- clocks: four clocks, in the order of clock-names
- clock-names: "pae_clk", "pae_hclk_gate", "multi_pae_clk",
  "pae_adpt_clk"

The encoder is a child of a simple-bus. DMA addresses are
DDR-relative in a 512 MiB window at 1 KiB granularity, which is the
dma-ranges property already on that bus.

Example:

	encoder {
		compatible = "fullhan,fh8626-pae",
			     "fullhan,fh8626v100-encoder";
		reg-names = "core", "buffer", "sram";
		interrupts = <4>;
		clock-names = "pae_clk", "pae_hclk_gate",
			      "multi_pae_clk", "pae_adpt_clk";
	};
