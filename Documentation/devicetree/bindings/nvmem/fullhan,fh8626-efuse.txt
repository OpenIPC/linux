Fullhan FH8626 eFuse

Required properties:
- compatible: "fullhan,fh8626v100-efuse", "fullhan,fh8626-efuse"
- reg: MMIO window of the controller
- clocks: the eFuse clock
- clock-names: "efuse"

Example:

	efuse@f1600000 {
		compatible = "fullhan,fh8626v100-efuse",
			     "fullhan,fh8626-efuse";
		reg = <0xf1600000 0x4000>;
		clocks = <&clk FH8626_CLK_EFUSE>;
		clock-names = "efuse";
	};
